# Team & Participant Guide

Participation in the Cyberpunk Game Buildathon is **free** — no entry fee or bank-slip upload is required. Your team is confirmed as soon as you register.

## 1. Register Your Team

1. Open the event home page and click **Register Now** (or go to `/register`).
2. Enter your **Team Name**, **Leader Full Name**, login **Email**, and a **Password** (8–72 characters).
3. Add your remaining teammates (**2 to 4 members total**, including the team leader, each with a unique email address).
4. Submit **Create team account**. Your registration is confirmed right away and you are taken directly to your Team Dashboard (`/team`).

> Registration is only open during the published registration window. Once registration closes, existing teams can still sign in at `/login`, manage their submission, and view their roster.

## 2. Check Confirmation on Your Team Dashboard (`/team`)

Sign in anytime at **Team Login** (`/login`) to open your Team Dashboard:

- **Registration Status**: Displays **Registration Confirmed** (`Officially Registered` / `Free Entry`).
- **Game Submission Status**: Shows whether your team has submitted a game yet and provides a direct link to `/team/submission`.
- **Team Members & Roster**: Lists your team leader and all registered team members.

*(Note: Any old `/team/payment` bookmarks now show a notice confirming registration is free and direct you back to your Team Dashboard.)*

## 3. Edit Team or Member Details (Before Cutoff)

While registration is open:

- Click **Edit Team Info** to update your **Team Name** or **Leader Full Name** (your leader login email stays fixed to your account).
- Click **+ Add Member** to add a teammate (up to **4 members** total).
- Click **Edit** next to a teammate to update their name or email address.
- Click **Remove** to drop a teammate (teams must keep at least **2 members** total).
- Reload the page anytime to confirm saved details directly from the server.

Once **registration closes**, roster editing is automatically locked (`Roster Locked`) so team names and members remain fixed for the event.

## 4. Submit Your Game (`/team/submission`)

When submissions are open, click **Go to Game Submission** from the top navigation or dashboard banner to enter:

- **Game Title** & **Description**
- **GitHub Repository URL** (`https://github.com/...`)
- Optional **Playable Game URL** and **Demo Video URL**

You can save and update your submission as needed until the submission window closes.
